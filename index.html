Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SavoLife collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SavoLife mobile application.

SavoLife is a personal finance and expense tracking app designed to help users record, organise, and understand their daily spending and lifestyle records.

By using SavoLife, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SavoLife may collect, store, or process the following types of information:

  • Expense and transaction data: such as amounts, dates, categories, accounts, notes, and other records you manually create in the app.
  • Receipt information: if you choose to use receipt scanning features, the app may process receipt images, extracted receipt text, merchant names, dates, amounts, and line-item details.
  • User-provided app context: such as category names, tag names, store names, source names, and other labels you create in the app. This information may be used to improve receipt scanning results.
  • Subscription and purchase information: such as your subscription status or in-app purchase information provided through Apple App Store services.
  • Device and app information: such as device identifiers, app version, operating system version, and technical information needed to operate and protect the app.
  • Account information: if login, account linking, cloud backup, or syncing features are enabled, we may process information related to your account or device installation.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve SavoLife. This includes:

  • Saving and displaying your expense records.
  • Providing receipt scanning or automatic data extraction features.
  • Managing subscriptions and premium feature access.
  • Supporting cloud backup, syncing, or account-related features where available.
  • Improving app reliability, security, and user experience.
  • Responding to support requests or user enquiries.

3. Local Storage

SavoLife may store your expense records and app data locally on your device. Local data remains on your device unless you choose to use features that require cloud services, backup, syncing, receipt scanning, or account-related functionality.

4. AI Receipt Scanning

If you choose to use AI receipt scanning, SavoLife sends your receipt image and related app context to SavoLife servers and third-party AI service providers to extract transaction details.

Related app context may include category names, tag names, store names, source names, and other labels you have created in the app. This helps the AI receipt scanning feature match extracted receipt details to your existing records.

AI receipt scanning is optional. You can choose not to use this feature and continue using manual expense tracking features.

Before your first AI receipt scan, the app asks for your explicit consent before sending receipt images or related app context to third-party AI service providers.

SavoLife uses receipt images and related app context only to provide the receipt scanning feature. SavoLife does not sell your personal information and does not use receipt images for advertising or tracking.

SavoLife does not intentionally store uploaded receipt images on our backend after the scan request is completed, unless retention is required for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or troubleshooting requested by you.

5. Cloud Services and Third-Party Providers

Some features may require the use of third-party services. These services may process information only as necessary to provide the relevant app features.

SavoLife may use services including, but not limited to:

  • Apple App Store services for subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Backend or cloud service providers for data storage, backup, syncing, account management, app functionality, security, and server-side processing.
  • Third-party AI service providers for receipt image recognition and transaction detail extraction, only when you choose to use AI receipt scanning.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies and service terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate SavoLife.
  • When necessary to provide features you choose to use.
  • When you give permission for optional features, such as AI receipt scanning.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of SavoLife, our users, or others.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the app, operate our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Data stored locally on your device may remain there until you delete it, uninstall the app, or reset the relevant app data.

Receipt images submitted for AI receipt scanning are processed to provide the scan result. SavoLife does not intentionally retain uploaded receipt images after the scan request is completed, unless retention is required for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or troubleshooting requested by you.

8. Data Deletion

You may delete records within the app where supported. If cloud, account, subscription-related, or support-related data is stored by us, you may contact us to request deletion of your data where applicable.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legitimate business purposes.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

SavoLife is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. International Data Processing

If cloud services or third-party providers are used, your information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure that information is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

12. Your Choices

You can choose not to use optional features that require data to be sent off your device, such as AI receipt scanning. You can continue using manual expense tracking features without using AI receipt scanning.

You may also contact us to ask questions about your data, request deletion where applicable, or ask about privacy choices available for SavoLife.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of SavoLife after any changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
